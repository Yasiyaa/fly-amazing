# Sri Lanka Inbound Tours (Amazing World Travel relaunch): Recon Brief

Date: 2026-11-02. Prices are published "from" figures, per person (pp), as fetched on this date. Sources marked (stale) are more than 12 months old.

## Verdict
**Build.** The big Colombo operators (Jetwing, Walkers, Aitken Spence, Blue Lanka) hide their prices and only take enquiries. UK/US specialists show USD prices, but at 2–3x local rates. A site with clear USD prices, online deposits and an AI planner grounded in the real catalogue has no direct competitor. The binding constraint is payments: **Stripe cannot be used by a Sri Lanka-registered business**.

## Market
- 2025 set a record of 2.36M arrivals (+15.1%). Top markets: India 27%, UK 9%, Russia 8%, Germany 6%, China 6%. The 2026 target is 3M [28]. The site should be global, not Australia-first.

## Booking norms
- **Deposits:** 20% with the balance 90 days out [14], up to 50% with the balance 7 days before arrival [16]. Group brands take a flat US$400 with the balance at 56 days [19].
- **Cancellation (typical ladder):** 91+ days, deposit lost; 61–90 days, 30%; 31–60, 60%; 15–30, 90%; 0–14, 100% [14].
- **Stripe: NO.** Sri Lanka is not on Stripe's 51-country list [1]. Workarounds need a foreign entity, so take legal and FX advice first.
- **Rails that work:**
  - **PayHere USD payouts:** needs a Seylan BFC account and a Sri Lanka-registered business. Plus plan: 3.99%, max US$1,000 per payment. Premium plan: 3.69%, max US$5,000 per payment [2]. Larger payments need splitting.
  - **ComBank IPG (Mastercard Gateway):** dynamic currency conversion since Dec 2025 [6]. **Sampath IPG:** multi-network [7].
  - **PayPal:** live since 15 May 2026 for receiving international payments via BOC, ComBank and Sampath [4]. Sampath pays out in LKR or USD [5]. Whether customers can pay a business directly by PayPal checkout is unconfirmed.
  - **Bank transfer (SWIFT):** still the norm for balances [16].

## AI trip planner
- **Competitors:** Walkers is the only one with an AI chatbot. It is undated and no results are published [22]. None of the other seven sites shows one [11][12][13][15][17][18].
- **Demand and trust:** 91% of travellers use AI for planning, but only 35% fully trust it [23]. 30% of travel advisors say clients arrive with AI-made itineraries [24].
- **Where generic AI fails:** 90% of ChatGPT itineraries contained errors, 24% included closed venues and 52% scheduled visits outside opening hours [25] (stale, 2024). In a 2025 test, Mindtrip misread trip dates and Gemini was the most accurate but gave no booking links [26] (stale, 2025). AI also ignores seasonality, fatigue and mobility needs [23].
- **How ours stands out:** answer only from our own catalogue and policies, and link to the package pages. Use real drive times and monsoon logic. Return a priced USD itinerary. Hand off to a human on WhatsApp and to the deposit checkout. Never invent prices or availability.

## User pain points
- Commission stops: "your driver gets a commission on everything you buy" [33].
- AI planners: "incorrectly told me the dates for my trip were in the past" [26].
- Every major local DMC hides its prices [10][11][12][15].

## Trust signals
- **SLTDA registration** is legally required [29] (stale, 2018). Show the licence number.
- **SLAITO** is the association of the main inbound operators [31] (stale, 2014).
- **What competitors lead with:** TripAdvisor or Feefo badges [8][15], Travelife/GSTC [12][13], World Travel Awards [10][15]. ATOL applies only to UK sellers [8].
- **Client gaps:** Amazing World Travel showed no review footprint in search on 2026-11-02. www.amazingworldtravel.com fails TLS (the certificate covers only the apex domain), and the apex page is titled "FlyAmazing" [34].

## Gaps / wedges (ranked)
1. Published USD prices plus an online deposit at local-DMC rates. No local operator does both.
2. Grounded AI planner that hands off to a human and to the deposit checkout.
3. A "No commission stops" pledge plus video profiles of vetted chauffeur-guides.
4. Video-first catalogue fed by admin uploads.
5. Season-aware routing, including an east-coast summer product.

## Risks
- **Payments:** no Stripe, PayHere per-payment caps, unclear PayPal consumer scope.
- **AI liability:** invented prices or availability.
- **Trust:** no reviews yet.
- **Pricing:** FX and seasonal hotel rates shift.

## Recommendations for the spec
1. Charge in USD. Display GBP, EUR, AUD and INR. Replace the AUD pricing in Tour Architect.
2. Take a 25% deposit online, with the balance due 60 days before arrival. Copy the cancellation ladder from [14].
3. Put payments behind a provider interface. v1: ComBank or Sampath IPG, with PayHere Premium as fallback and PayPal as an alternative. No Stripe.
4. AI agent: retrieval over the package/hotel/destination data plus policies. Tools: `estimatePrice`, `createEnquiry`, `holdBooking`. WhatsApp handoff, transcripts visible in admin.
5. Admin area: package CRUD to replace the static `src/data` arrays, plus image/video upload with transcoding.
6. Trust block in the footer and checkout: SLTDA number, SLAITO (if a member), review widget, guide profiles.
7. Fix the www TLS certificate before launch.
